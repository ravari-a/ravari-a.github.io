Amirhossein Ravari

PhD Candidate · Electrical & Computer Engineering, Northeastern University

Boston, MA

ravari.a [at] northeastern [dot] edu

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. Mahdi Imani in the Imani Lab. My research focuses on reinforcement learning, inverse reinforcement learning, Bayesian inference, and human–AI collaboration. I develop methods that learn models of human decision-making and expert knowledge from limited behavioral data, with applications in cyber defense, disaster response, and the biomedical sciences.

I have ten peer-reviewed publications, eight as first author, in venues including Artificial Intelligence, IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence, IEEE Control Systems Letters, L4DC, ACC, CCTA, Asilomar, and ICASSP. Recent work includes personalized models of human decision-making in cyber defense, posterior-conditioned multi-agent reinforcement learning for adaptive cyber defense, and inference of latent task models in human–AI collaboration.

In 2025, I was a LEADERs Fellow at Northeastern working with the startup Walkky, where I co-developed RhythmBERT, a self-supervised ECG language model, and researched multimodal language models that combine ECG signals, echocardiograms, and clinical reports. Before Northeastern, I completed my M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology.

Open to opportunities for Spring and Summer 2027. I am looking for research internships and full-time positions in machine learning, across applied research, ML engineering, and quantitative finance. My CV is here; feel free to reach out.
