I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/), advised by Prof. [Mahdi Imani](https://imani.lab.northeastern.edu/) in the Imani Lab. My research focuses on **reinforcement learning**, **inverse reinforcement learning**, **Bayesian inference**, and **human–AI collaboration**. I develop methods that learn models of human decision-making and expert knowledge from limited behavioral data, with applications in cyber defense, disaster response, and the biomedical sciences.

I have ten peer-reviewed publications, eight as first author, in venues including *Artificial Intelligence*, *IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence*, *IEEE Control Systems Letters*, L4DC, ACC, CCTA, Asilomar, and ICASSP. Recent work includes personalized models of human decision-making in cyber defense, posterior-conditioned multi-agent reinforcement learning for adaptive cyber defense, and inference of latent task models in human–AI collaboration.

In 2025, I was a LEADERs Fellow at Northeastern working with the startup Walkky, where I co-developed RhythmBERT, a self-supervised ECG language model, and researched multimodal language models that combine ECG signals, echocardiograms, and clinical reports. Before Northeastern, I completed my M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.sharif.edu/).
